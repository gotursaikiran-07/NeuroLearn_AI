# Learnova AI — Hackathon Demonstration Script & Pitch Guide

## 🎙️ 1-Minute Elevator Pitch

> "Traditional learning platforms present identical content to every single student — ignoring whether someone is struggling with basic concepts or ready for advanced edge cases.
> 
> **Learnova AI** is an intelligent learning companion that adapts to how a student thinks. It diagnoses exact misconceptions, transforms lessons into stories or technical breakdowns in one click, reads explanations aloud, and dynamically adapts question difficulty based on student performance.
> 
> Today, we're demonstrating Learnova AI with a student learning **DBMS & SQL JOINs** for technical interview preparation."

---

## 🎬 Step-by-Step Live Judge Walkthrough (3 Minutes)

### Phase 1: The Problem & Onboarding (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Action:** Open Learnova AI landing page. Click **"Instant Judge Demo"** in the top navigation bar.
- **Narrative:** *"With one click, our platform preloads a full student journey. Alex is a beginner studying DBMS for an upcoming software engineering interview."*

### Phase 2: Diagnostic Assessment & Misconception Analysis (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Action:** Open the **Diagnostic Assessment** tab. Answer Question 2 incorrectly (selecting option A instead of NULL).
- **Narrative:** *"Notice what happens when Alex chooses an incorrect answer. The system doesn't just say 'Wrong'. It pinpoints the exact misconception: confusing LEFT JOIN's NULL preservation with an empty string, and updates the student's mastery score in real time."*

### Phase 3: AI Learning Studio & Multi-Style Adaptability (1:30 - 2:15)
- **Action:** Navigate to **AI Studio**. Click **"Explain with a Story"**, then click **"Read Lesson Aloud"**, then type a question in **Ask Learnova AI**.
- **Narrative:** *"In the AI Learning Studio, Alex can transform the SQL JOIN explanation into 'Story Mode' — comparing tables to King Relational's citizen banquet scrolls. With built-in Web Speech TTS, students with auditory learning preferences can listen to the lesson, and ask contextual follow-ups in our side AI panel."*

### Phase 4: Intelligent Practice Arena & Gap Remediation (2:15 - 3:00)
- **Action:** Open **Practice Arena** -> answer 2 items correctly to see the difficulty badge scale from `Easy` to `Medium`. Then open **Gaps** to highlight the **"Fix This Gap"** remediation button.
- **Narrative:** *"In the Practice Arena, question difficulty automatically scales up as accuracy increases. Finally, our Knowledge Gap Detector categorizes every concept into clear evidence tiers, offering 1-click remediation shortcuts so students always know what to study next."*
